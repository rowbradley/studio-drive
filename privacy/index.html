Privacy Policy — studio-drive

Last updated: November 21, 2026

studio-drive is a private, single-user command-line backup utility operated by Studio Wild Co (studiowildco@gmail.com) to transfer personal media and archive files to the owner's own Google Drive account using rclone.

Data accessed

Using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, the app only creates, reads, verifies, and manages files that it uploads itself. It does not access, list, or read any other files in Google Drive.

Storage and sharing

No user data is collected, logged to third-party servers, sold, or shared with anyone. OAuth credentials and file transfer logs are stored locally on the owner's private machines only.

Revocation and contact

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Questions: studiowildco@gmail.com.
